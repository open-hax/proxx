## `:spec/schemas`

Five malli schemas forming a closed type hierarchy:

- **`:proxx/ctx`** — the immutable ground truth fed into every node. Matches your pseudo-code shape exactly, including the `:credentials` vector.
- **`:proxx/eval-op`** — the five quantifiers: `:all` `:some` `:none` `:not` `:assert`. `:assert` replaces `:identity` — it names what it actually does (single terminal predicate).
- **`:proxx/eval-node`** — the recursive AND-OR tree node. `:eval/target` is optional; when present it binds a key from ctx as implicit `it` inside forms so your filter predicates don't have to reach through the whole ctx manually.
- **`:proxx/policy`** — the three-phase structure you derived: condition (gate) → filters (narrow) → outcome (effect). `:policy/strategy` is a qualified symbol resolved at eval time, keeping the EDN data-pure.
- **`:proxx/trace-entry`** — the write-only side channel. Never read by eval-node. Present as a record type so your observability layer has something typed to append to.

## `:spec/interpreter`

Six laws that make the interpreter **correct by contract**, not by testing every case:

1. Trace is write-only
2. `nil` = failure, always
3. `:some` is the choice point — the backtracking mechanism
4. `:all` is the conjunction — fails fast, doesn't backtrack
5. `:contract/kind :strategy` is the phase boundary into effect space
6. Filters are AND-composed before children are attempted
